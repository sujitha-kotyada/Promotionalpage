Beautiful Crafts. Limited Reach.

Across India, talented artisans create products rooted in generations of skill and tradition. Yet many small sellers struggle to reach customers outside their local markets.

Limited Market Access

Many artisans depend on local buyers and word of mouth.

Digital Selling Barriers

Small businesses may lack the skills or resources to establish an online presence.

Low Product Visibility

Regional crafts can remain undiscovered by customers who would value them.

The talent already exists. SakhiSell aims to help it reach further.
